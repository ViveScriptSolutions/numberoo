## 1. Overview and Core Commitment

ViveScript Solutions LLC ("we", "our", or "us") developed **Numberoo - Learn Count Add Subtract** as a playful, child-friendly educational math utility designed for young children and families.

We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of young learners and their families. **Numberoo does not collect, track, harvest, or store any personal information from its users.** The application operates completely offline on your device for all educational math activities.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in radical data minimization:

* **No Accounts or User Profiles:** You do not create an account, log in, or provide any name, email, or credentials to use the app.
* **No Personal Data:** We do not collect names, ages, phone numbers, physical addresses, or email addresses.
* **No Precise or Coarse Location:** The application does not request or access location permissions (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` or `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`).
* **No Sensor or Media Data:** The app does not access your microphone, camera, contacts, or storage files.
* **No Analytics or Trackers:** We do not include third-party tracking SDKs (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Adjust, AppsFlyer, Facebook SDK).
* **No Behavioral Profiling:** We never build user profiles, track children across apps, or engage in targeted behavioral advertising.

---

## 3. Local On-Device Storage

To support an engaging learning journey, the app stores a minimal set of non-personal preferences directly on the user's local device via Android `SharedPreferences`:

1. **Stars Collected:** The number of stars earned by answering math questions correctly.
2. **Sound Preferences:** Mute / Unmute state.
3. **Language Selection:** English or Bangla (বাংলা) interface setting.
4. **Questions Solved Counter:** Total count of completed activities.

This data never leaves the device and is never transmitted to any external server or cloud service. It is erased automatically if the user uninstalls the application or clears app storage.

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy Compliance)

Numberoo is designed primarily for young children (ages 3–8). We strictly adhere to the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**, the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K)**, and Google Play's **Families Policy**:

* We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
* All educational features work without requiring an internet connection.
* There are no social sharing buttons, chat features, multiplayer modes, or open web browser links accessible to children during learning.

## 5. Advertising and Monetization

Numberoo is currently configured using **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) official test identifiers**:

* **Application ID:** `ca-app-pub-3940256099942544~3347511713` (Google official test ID)
* **Banner Ad Unit ID:** `ca-app-pub-3940256099942544/6300978111` (Google official test ID)

In accordance with Google Play Families Policy and COPPA guidelines, all ad requests are strictly tagged with:
* `setTagForChildDirectedTreatment(TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT_TRUE)`
* `setTagForUnderAgeOfConsent(TAG_FOR_UNDER_AGE_OF_CONSENT_TRUE)`
* `setMaxAdContentRating(MAX_AD_CONTENT_RATING_G)`

This ensures that only non-personalized, family-safe, G-rated advertisements can ever be requested, with no behavioral tracking, user profiling, or remarketing.

## 6. Security

Because Numberoo does not transmit or store personal information on remote servers, the risk of data compromise is inherently eliminated. Local application data resides exclusively within Android's sandboxed private app storage.
